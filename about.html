About Albumy

Albumy is a minimal photo organizer for static hosting. It helps you keep albums grouped by date, reorder them quickly, and preview photos in clean tiles.

Why this style?

The interface uses a sleek-retro theme: dark surfaces, bright amber/cyan accents, and bold focus states. It is designed to feel nostalgic while staying readable and accessible.

How it works

  • Photos are loaded from a configured same-origin path (default /photos).
  • Album and photo metadata are read from manifest.json.
  • Album reordering is persisted in browser localStorage.
